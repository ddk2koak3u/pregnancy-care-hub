## 移植后饮食原则

 移植后的饮食应该以清淡、易消化为主，选择富含蛋白质、维生素、矿物质的食物。避免辛辣刺激、油腻煎炸食物，也不要饮酒或咖啡等刺激性饮料。

 Post-transplantation diet should focus on light and easily digestible foods, choosing those rich in protein, vitamins, and minerals. Avoid spicy, stimulating, greasy fried foods, and refrain from consuming alcohol or caffeinated beverages.
